About Me

My name is Daniel Akinseye

Even though I may not have had many opportunities to use my talent yet, I believe that God has a purpose and a time for everything. I will continue to trust Him, serve Him, and remain committed to my calling.

My desire is not just to use my talent, but to use it to touch lives, lead people closer to God, and make His name known through my ministry.

I am Daniel Akinseye — a servant of God, and I will continue to serve Him with the gift He has given me.

My Ministry

I am a gospel minister who sings praise and worship songs and also composes songs. Through music, I desire to worship God, encourage people, and help others draw closer to Him.

I believe the talent God has given me is a gift, and I want to use it to glorify Him and serve His purpose.
